Our Story

Where Tradition
Meets Uganda

Holland Cheese was founded with a simple mission: to introduce Uganda to the authentic taste of premium cheeses while supporting local farmers.

A dairy cheese factory launched in Kampala in July 2012, Holland Cheese is a brand of cheeses made from cow's milk from Friesian and Ankole cows. The range includes all traditional and specialty types of Hard Cheeses, as well as a number of soft cheeses — all made from fresh milk sourced from local farmers.

Our cheeses are naturally ripened and crafted by experienced cheese makers to provide the pure goodness and authentic taste of "nature's milk at its best."

Holland Cheese uses premium ingredients to ensure its authentic cheeses taste consistently superior. Three key factors are essential: the origin of ingredients, craftsmanship, and natural organic goodness.

Craftsmanship

Led by a
World-Class Master

Holland Cheese has a team of cheese makers headed by our Cheese Master Klaas Zomerdijk from Holland, with over 65 years of experience producing and building cheese factories all over the world, and numerous cheese tasting awards to his name.

As we have delivered the best quality cheese products on the East African market for over 14 years and counting, our commitment to quality has never wavered.

Today we offer more than 10 varieties — from classic Hard Cheeses to Mozzarella and Feta not to mention but a few — all selected for quality and exceptional flavour.
